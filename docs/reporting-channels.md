# Reporting channels and rewards (Thailand)

Research notes from 2026-10-20. Most sources are news and blog articles, not official notices.
**Verify with 1197 / 1584 before relying on any detail**, and update this file with what you learn.

## Where to report

| Violation | Agency | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| Red light, solid-line lane change, wrong way, footpath riding, phone use, no helmet | Royal Thai Police, traffic | Hotline **1197**, police traffic LINE/Facebook channels |
| Public transport or commercial vehicles; black smoke from trucks/buses; modified vehicles (wheels past the body); overloading | Department of Land Transport (DLT, dlt.go.th) | Hotline **1584**, "1584" LINE and Facebook |
| Black smoke (pollution) | Pollution Control Department | Hotline **1650** (no reward found) |

## Rewards

- Several articles state the reporter receives **50 % of the fine after the state's share is deducted**, under the
  Land Traffic Act and, for DLT matters, the Motor Vehicle Act / Land Transport Act 1979 (DLT rule from 2019-12-09).
- When the case closes, the reporter gets an SMS and the money is transferred within about 15 days.
- Fines are often a few hundred baht, so a typical share is roughly 100–500 baht, and it is paid only if the owner pays.
- Some traffic pages and radio stations run clip contests instead (2025: 21 prizes, 110,000 baht in total).

## What good evidence needs

- The plate clearly readable, the date and time, the place, the vehicle, and the moment of the violation.
- Do not post clips publicly: it risks defamation and breaks the PDPA. Send to the agency only.
- Never handle the phone while driving (the app is built around this).
